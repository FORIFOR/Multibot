03 / A role for everyone

One makes.
Another checks.

Separate roles, with their conversation preserved. A reviewer can still miss errors; check important work yourself.

FAQ

Questions

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?+

It is not one AI answering once. Making and checking are separate jobs. You receive files, not a reply, along with a record of what was checked and what is still unverified.

Will the result always be right?+

No. The reviewer can miss things. That is why partial and unverified results are shown as they are, and why we publish the runs that went badly. Check important work yourself.

What does it cost?+

The app is free and open source (MIT). A cloud model bills you for usage. You can set a budget limit per request, and work stops when it is reached.

Can I sign up and start immediately?+

A public service is not available yet. You can currently install and try it in your own environment. Public hosting, self-registration, operations and business-quality acceptance are still in progress.

Where is my data stored?+

In the execution environment: your computer for a local installation, or the server for a dedicated installation. Choosing a cloud AI model sends input to that provider.
